Kahn Brothers Group

Kahn Brothers Group

How they invest Deep value · Obscure and out of favor · Long holding periods

Why I follow Kahn

Irving Kahn was Ben Graham's teaching assistant at Columbia and started the firm with his sons in 1978; he died in 2015, at 109. The firm still looks for the obscure and the out of favor, and Citigroup, Merck, Pfizer, BP and Exxon Mobil are in every 13F since 2013.
