What a 13F shows

Long positions in US-listed stocks, plus some options and ADRs, as of the last day of the quarter. Firms have 45 days to file, so the picture is up to six weeks old when it comes out. Short positions, cash, bonds and stocks listed outside the US don't appear, so treat it as a partial view. More on what 13Fs cover.
